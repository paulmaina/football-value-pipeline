# Asian handicap price-edge backtest (pre-registered rule)

Read-only. No production model, policy, threshold or workflow was changed.

Rule: on the opening line AHh, bet a side when offered odds x Pinnacle opening fair probability (proportional margin removal) - 1 >= 2%, at most one side per match, 1 unit flat at the offered odds. Betfair Exchange odds are net of 5% commission. CLV = odds x Pinnacle fair closing probability - 1, only where the closing line equals the opening line. 95% intervals resample whole gameweeks.

## Verdict

- **max** (best price across bookmakers (which bookmaker is not recorded)): +0.0246 [+0.0178, +0.0317] over 256 same-line bets -> positive CLV, interval above zero.
  - Shin check +0.0213 [+0.0143, +0.0282] -> positive CLV, interval above zero; 25/26 only +0.0162 [-0.0005, +0.0275] over 19 -> too few same-line bets to read (< 30).
- **b365** (Bet365): +0.0316 [+0.0111, +0.0503] over 17 same-line bets -> too few same-line bets to read (< 30).
- **bfe** (Betfair Exchange, odds net of commission): +0.0115 [-0.0358, +0.0420] over 9 same-line bets -> too few same-line bets to read (< 30).
- **avg** (market average (control: should not show an edge)): +0.0286 [+0.0224, +0.0348] over 2 same-line bets -> too few same-line bets to read (< 30).

Does the pre-registered rule show positive CLV with a 95% interval above zero? Yes for: max.
The market-average control almost never clears the edge (2 bets), as expected; there are too few bets to test its CLV.

History cannot show WHICH bookmaker offered the max price, or whether it would have accepted the stake, so the max-price result is an upper bound. Live tracking of quotes that can actually be taken is the real test. This backtest is not a recommendation to change production.
