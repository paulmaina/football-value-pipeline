The shortlist blocks both cases: name score < 90 or league average is rejected (`conservative_bet_filter.py:40,196-202`). But raw forecasts, readable reports and graded metrics carry them. **Severity MEDIUM** (wrong-team probabilities in published forecasts; no picks affected).

### 1.3 Odds

**D4 — Stale committed odds caches are treated as fresh after a CI checkout. HIGH.**
- `.football_cache/oddsapi_events_*.json` and `oddsapi_odds_ah_v1_*_eu.json` are git-tracked. The last manual commit (`a792adc7e`, 24 Oct) holds quotes last updated 2026-10-24 14:40–14:49 UTC.
- `CachedHttpClient.get_bytes` judges freshness by file mtime (`:322-328`), and a fresh checkout sets mtime to "now". The events cache name ignores the requested time window (`:770`, `:1088`).
- The CI run at 11:35–11:45 UTC on 26 Oct reused the 24 Oct responses for every league fetched within 10 minutes of checkout:
  - EPL, LaLiga, Bundesliga and Serie A got 22 of 25 fixtures with kickoffs on 24–25 Oct, i.e. **already played**. Ligue 1 and Primeira, fetched later, got fresh quotes.
  - The stale quotes failed the freshness check, so the 0.70 blend applied to **2 of 25 fixtures** in the current run.
  - One genuine upcoming fixture (Sevilla–Osasuna) was published as pure Dixon-Coles.
  - The training data already contained the results of the 22 played fixtures (`training_end` 2026-10-25), so `raw/` and `readable/*_report.md` currently show "forecasts" for completed matches, with those results in training.
- The training CSV path already guards against exactly this mtime problem (`:449-453`). The odds path does not.

Across all 54 snapshots, **9 runs** carried past-kickoff rows (148 rows in total), and 37 pre-kickoff rows were published unblended. Selection and snapshot capture both exclude past kickoffs, so **grading is not contaminated**. The losses are coverage and blend quality.

**D5 — The 1X2 and O/U 2.5 reference is "most recently updated book", not the sharpest. MEDIUM.**
- `ref_h2h = max(complete_h2h, key=last_update)` (`:1032-1034`) feeds `market_snapshot` (`:2366-2389`) and the 0.70 blend (`:2995-3002`).
- Reference shares across 3,120 rows: Marathon Bet 17.7%, Winamax DE 11.0%, **Pinnacle 7.8%**, William Hill 7.2%, Winamax FR 6.8%, Unibet FR 5.1%, and so on.
- Median reference margin is 5.25% (Pinnacle 3.76%). 369 rows (11.8%) used a reference with a margin above 10%.
- Mean |reference de-vig − Pinnacle fair close| is 1.8–2.6pp for most books, but 3.3–3.7pp for Betclic FR and PMU FR. Timing drift dominates: even a Pinnacle entry quote is 2.4pp from the close. The model's own gap to the close is 4.35pp.
- For O/U 2.5, Pinnacle was the reference in only 51 rows, because only exact-2.5 main lines qualify (see M2).

**Other odds checks:**
- **No opening/closing mix-up.** Live forecasts use current quotes. Historical forecasts use the opening triples first (`PSH` → `B365H` → `AvgH`). Closing columns (`PSC*`, `PC>2.5`) appear only in backtest/benchmark evaluation (`:3436`, `historical_benchmark.py:261-263`).
- **Implausible prices.** Books outside overround 0.98–1.20 are dropped (`:832-846`). 56 rows used a sub-1% reference (Betfair exchange, 1xBet), which is acceptable for de-vigging.
- **De-vig method.** Power method everywhere, with a proportional fallback when bisection fails to bracket (`:2238`).
- **Pinnacle coverage.** Live, Pinnacle is present in 212 of 213 cached events (99.5%) and in 77 of 77 per-event responses. Historically it is missing for 50% of 2025/26 and 100% of 2026/27 (D1).

**No closing odds or same-day results reach a graded forecast.** The only leak is D4's past-kickoff rows in the published raw and readable files.
